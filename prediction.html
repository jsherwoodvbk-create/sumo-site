How it works

  1. Predict where each of the 42 makuuchi wrestlers lands on the next banzuke.
  2. Your score is the rank-distance between each pick and where the wrestler actually ends up, added up. An exact call is a clean zero.
  3. Lowest total wins. Closest to the real banzuke takes it.
  4. Call the juryo wrestlers getting promoted for bonus points off your score.
  5. Picks lock and everything grades the moment the real banzuke is announced.
