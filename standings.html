Live data. Records pull straight from the Match Log in Notion, current through the latest day the daily sync has posted. Height & weight come from Master Rikishi and the basho banzuke, static facts, so they are never gated; only the W–L and the day-strip hide behind your watched day.

The dot after each name is the wrestler's mawashi (belt) color, logged in Master Rikishi: a faint ring means we haven't recorded one yet.

Reading it: the W–L bolds whichever side leads (both when tied). A row fills green with a ✓ KK the moment someone clinches kachi-koshi (8 wins), red with ✗ MK at make-koshi (8 losses). A wrestler sitting the basho out (kyujo) shows “out” and sorts to the bottom rather than a misleading 0–0.

The cup. When you're caught up to the final day, the tournament champion (yusho) gets the 🏆 Emperor's Cup banner and a purple row. If a playoff decided it, the cup names the winner the tied records can't.
